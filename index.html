Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pancham Seeds specialize in specific crops? expand_more
We offer a comprehensive range of both Vegetable crops (like Tomato, Brinjal, Chilli, Okra) and Field crops (like Cotton, Maize, Paddy). Our expertise spans across high-yield hybrid varieties and research-driven seeds tailored for regional performance.
How are seed germination and quality tested? expand_more
All our seeds undergo a multi-stage quality protocol in our certified laboratories and trial fields. We test for germination rates, genetic purity, seedling vigor, and disease resistance to ensure only the highest quality seeds reach our farmers.
How can I select the right variety for my soil type? expand_more
We recommend consulting our regional crop experts or visiting a Pancham authorized dealer. Our product catalog also provides detailed soil and climatic requirements for each variety to help you make an informed decision.
How can I become an authorized dealer? expand_more
Interested businesses can apply through our 'Dealer Inquiry' form on the website or contact our regional sales offices. We look for partners who share our commitment to agricultural excellence and farmer prosperity.
