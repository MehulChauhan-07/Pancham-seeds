Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pancham Seeds specialize in specific crops? expand_more
We offer a comprehensive range of both Vegetable crops (like Tomato, Brinjal, Chilli, Okra) and Field crops (like Cotton, Maize, Paddy). Our expertise spans across high-yield hybrid varieties and research-driven seeds tailored for regional performance.
How are seed germination and quality tested? expand_more
All our seeds undergo a multi-stage quality protocol in our certified laboratories and trial fields. We test for germination rates, genetic purity, seedling vigor, and disease resistance.
